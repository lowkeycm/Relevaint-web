## 1. Brand in one sentence

Relevaint helps established small businesses improve their sales and marketing with better websites, compelling content, and connected systems that support the journey from first interest to follow-up.

**Category:** Sales and marketing partner.
**Descriptive specialty:** Websites, creative, and connected sales and marketing systems.
**Central idea:** Make the pieces work together.

“Ecosystem” can describe a complete engagement, but specific services and business problems should explain the offer first.

## 2. Purpose, promise, and point of view

**Purpose:** Help good businesses improve how they present themselves, connect with customers, and follow through.

**Promise:** Help shape an agreed direction, build the pieces needed to execute it, and support their continued use and improvement within the agreed scope.

**Point of view:** The quality of each piece matters, and so does what happens between them. A compelling video needs a useful next step. A website needs a clear offer and a working inquiry path. A new lead needs to reach the right person with the right context.

The brand combines creative quality with operational understanding. It should make room for both an excellent standalone project and a connected build.

**Meaning of the name:** Relevaint began with helping businesses stay relevant as AI opened new possibilities. Its meaning now includes applying those possibilities thoughtfully to the work of attracting and serving customers.

AI is a genuine capability and part of the delivery approach. It does not need to be the customer’s motivation for buying. Explain its role where relevant without making every service “AI-powered.”

## 3. Ideal customer

An established small business with customers, revenue, and a worthwhile offer, whose sales and marketing have room to catch up with the business.

The business may have a website, a CRM, social accounts, and people handling inquiries, yet still operate in organized chaos. Its tools and processes may have accumulated independently. There is enough going right to build on, and enough friction to make improvement worthwhile.

The decision-maker can be the owner or a responsible manager. Industry is secondary to the situation, working relationship, and scope. Automotive experience is evidence of capability; dealerships are not the chosen niche.

**Likely situations to validate in discovery:**
- The business’s website or content no longer represents the quality of its work.
- Marketing activity happens inconsistently because production takes too much effort.
- Leads arrive, but follow-up depends on someone remembering.
- Customer information sits across tools, inboxes, or individual employees.
- The owner knows something needs to change but needs help deciding and executing the next step.

**Buying trigger:** “We could be doing this better. I need someone who understands what we’re trying to accomplish and can help get it done.”

**Good-fit behaviors:** Willingness to adjust, access to needed information, timely decisions, realistic expectations, and a designated person responsible for internal adoption.

**Poor fit:** Expecting different results while refusing to change; wanting unlimited work under a small support agreement; requiring enterprise-scale legacy transformation; or expecting Relevaint to take over staff management.

No fixed employee count or revenue threshold is established in this platform. Qualify by need, readiness, project complexity, and ability to fund the agreed work.

## 5. Positioning and differentiation

**Internal positioning statement**

For established small businesses ready to improve their sales and marketing, Relevaint is a hands-on partner that shapes and executes an agreed direction through websites, creative, funnels, and connected sales tools. Clay brings experience managing people and business processes alongside practical implementation, helping each project fit the way the business works.

**Reasons to choose Relevaint**
1. Business context informs the build. Understand the offer, customer, team, and process before choosing the solution.
2. Creative and operational thinking meet in one engagement. The presentation and the follow-through both receive attention.
3. Hands-on execution. Relevaint helps turn the agreed direction into usable work.
4. Flexible entry points. Start with one project or connect several components.
5. Continuity. Ongoing relationships can cover fresh creative, improvements, and care for agreed systems.

These are differentiating themes to demonstrate through work and client experience, not claims that no competitor offers them.

## 7. Ways to work together

**Focused project:** A defined website, video package, funnel, workflow, or integration. It should deliver standalone value.

**Connected build:** Multiple components designed around an agreed customer journey, such as creative, a landing page, lead capture, CRM routing, and follow-up.

**Ongoing partnership:** A continuing relationship combining planned improvements, fresh creative, and agreed support. Work is prioritized together.

Within recurring agreements, distinguish:
- **Care:** Agreed checks, maintenance, troubleshooting, and fixes for supported systems.
- **Improvement:** New creative, refinements, experiments, additional workflows, and other scoped work.

Maintenance supports the offer. Do not invent busywork to justify a retainer or imply that every website requires constant intervention.

**Internal commercial context:** Current stated rate is $185/hour. Founder described interest in lighter arrangements around $2,000–$3,000/month and more involved relationships around $5,000–$10,000/month. These are planning inputs, not approved public packages. Price against scope, capacity, support expectations, third-party costs, and responsibility. Do not publish “unlimited access.”

## 8. Working method and responsibilities

1. **Understand:** Review the business, customers, existing assets, tools, and friction.
2. **Agree:** Shape the direction together; decide priorities, deliverables, responsibilities, and measures.
3. **Build:** Create and connect the agreed components, with appropriate review.
4. **Launch:** Verify the customer journey, prepare the handoff, and guide the people using it.
5. **Improve:** Review useful evidence and prioritize the next changes when retained.

Relevaint contributes recommendations and challenges weak assumptions. The client participates in setting strategy and approves business decisions.

The client’s owner or manager owns employee accountability and adoption. Relevaint can guide implementation and train or support users within scope.

Paid-media buying, bidding, daily campaign management, daily community management, outsourced sales closing, and full change-management responsibility are not default inclusions. Do not imply them through vague “full-service” language.

For every engagement, define what success means, how it will be observed, and which factors remain with the client or other providers.

## 9. Brand personality and voice

**Personality:** Capable, approachable, observant, direct, creative, and grounded.

Write like an experienced person explaining useful work to a business owner. Use specific situations, natural sentences, and familiar language. Confidence should come from clarity and evidence.

| Prefer | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| “Connect website inquiries to your CRM and follow-up.” | “Unlock a seamless AI-powered growth ecosystem.” |
| “We’ll agree on what needs improving and build from there.” | “We transform every aspect of your business.” |
| “Start with the website, the follow-up, or both.” | Implying every client needs a complete rebuild |
| Explain what automation does and when a person steps in | “Put your entire business on autopilot.” |
| Show a relevant project and its actual role | Guaranteed leads, revenue, or conversion claims |

Use “we” for the company’s delivery and “I” where Clay speaks personally. Make the founder’s involvement clear without implying a large in-house team.

Humor can be dry and light when appropriate. Avoid constant attacks on AI vendors or agencies. Practical judgment should create confidence.

“Stay relevant” can support the brand story. It is too broad to explain the offer on its own.

## 12. Website and visual implications

The site should let customers recognize their needs, see the quality of the work, and understand the relationship.

**Recommended content order:** Clear offer → relevant work → recognizable customer problems → service entry points → how components connect → working process and founder credibility → contact.

Show individual service entry points alongside connected projects. Avoid making prospects learn the term “ecosystem” before they can understand or buy something.

**Proposed visual principles, pending design research:**
- Combine strong creative presentation with clear, easy-to-use navigation.
- Let actual websites, video, and workflow examples provide depth and credibility.
- Use motion where it reveals work or demonstrates a connection.
- Keep contact options readable and accessible throughout.
- Avoid stock futuristic AI imagery and technology logos as substitutes for proof.

Colors, typography, logo treatment, and motion direction remain to be selected. This platform does not claim completed design research or authorize a final visual system.

## 13. Guardrails for future collaborators

- Use this platform as the current strategic foundation; the old AI-consultancy website is historical context.
- Keep sales and marketing as the primary scope. Broad operational expertise supports delivery.
- Offer standalone projects as well as connected engagements.
- Present video directly as a Relevaint service.
- Shape and execute direction with the client; do not imply unilateral control of their marketing strategy.
- Keep client adoption responsibility with their owner or manager.
- Distinguish ongoing improvement from maintenance and define both.
- Use AI where useful without making enthusiasm for AI a qualification requirement.
- Preserve natural, specific language and substantiate results.
- Treat proposed copy and visual principles as recommendations until selected.
