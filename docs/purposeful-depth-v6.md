# Relevaint: purposeful depth revision

## The end in mind
A busy business owner should understand the work, see relevant examples quickly, and choose one service or the connected system. The user rejected v5's illustrative business photos and isolated collage treatment. Withdraw that visual acceptance: they added atmosphere but did not demonstrate CRM or follow-up. No new generic stock or generated photographs in this revision.

## Selected execution, before source edits
Use the supplied Skiper49 coverflow interaction with the existing three commercial posters. Preserve its attribution. Compact landscape cards occupy a short section; swiping, arrows and named choices browse them. Selecting a card opens a properly labeled shadcn dialog with a large native video player. Closing unmounts the player and stops audio. No autoplay carousel or background downloads. Adapt the component's props rather than importing unrelated demo illustrations. Project already has shadcn, Tailwind and TypeScript, UI path components/ui, primary styles app/globals.css.

Replace CRM/follow-up photos with functional explanatory interfaces labeled Example. These are interactive demonstrations of a workflow, not screenshots of a delivered CRM or evidence of measured client results. CRM: incoming inquiry + conversation + agreed next step become one readable customer record; selecting each reveals the useful information. Follow-up: select inquiry, AI-assisted draft, team review, customer response; show which work is automated and which belongs to a person. Never simulate sending messages.

Replace the homepage/full-system photo collage with a connected handoff diagram using real creative/website assets and semantic information panels. Each layer names its business job and links to its service.

Apply a coherent surface system across the site: recessed forest ground, raised ivory content planes, restrained copper edges and visible section overlap. Homepage service options stack as distinct readable sheets on native scroll. Service navigation bridges hero and problem; CRM choice panels have physical hierarchy; website outcomes and connected steps overlap rather than becoming repeated flat lists; inquiry/contact panels settle on their own foreground surface. Keep the existing approved neighborhood hero and existing business content. Avoid adding decorative noise to every blank space.
